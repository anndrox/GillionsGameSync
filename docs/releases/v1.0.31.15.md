# Gillions Game Sync 1.0.31.15 (Stable)

## What's new

- Hunt Progress puts the mark you have targeted in game at the top. Next comes the mark the website sent a map point for, then the mark that most recently gained a kill. The other marks stay in bill order.
- Each Hunt Progress row now fits on one line: the mark, then how many are left and the count.
- A mark you finish drops below the unfinished marks, then disappears after the short completion delay.

## Updating

- Update Game Sync through the Stable feed in Dalamud. Your pairing and sharing choices carry over.
- Keep just one Game Sync channel enabled.
- Rolling back to Stable 1.0.31.14 keeps your pairing. Rolling back to 1.0.31.13 or earlier still leaves you unpaired, as the 1.0.31.14 notes explain.
