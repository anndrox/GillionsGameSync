## What a 1.0.31.2 user gets

- **Current game build.** Hunt Progress and Hunt reads support game
  `2026.10.16.0000.0000` (Dalamud 15.0.3.9, ClientStructs 7.57.0.9414) through the
  per-collector compatibility table. **Hunts no longer show "Update required".**
- **Data protection** (PR #11): unavailable inventory, currencies, gil and
  character data are withheld, never sent as empty or zero. Unopened saddlebags
  are no longer deleted on the website.
- **Clear status** (Testing 0.0.91 and 0.0.92):
  - Each feature row names its real condition: game version not supported yet,
    Off, Off on Gillions, Not available on Gillions, waiting, temporarily
    unavailable, or ready.
  - Gillions refusals are named instead of "Gillions unavailable": character not
    linked, paired to a different character, reconnect required, or refused.
    The two character problems also get a one-time in-game chat notice.
  - Party Finder no longer always shows Ready.
- **Reliability** (Testing 0.0.91):
  - fewer full-configuration saves;
  - no per-frame retries;
  - stable retainer attribution;
  - item links no longer blocked by the Party Finder check;
  - Reconnect → Finish no longer stacks windows.
- **Less traffic** (Testing 0.0.93 and 0.0.94):
  - retainer data uploads on change, plus a 5-minute refresh;
  - inventory uploads are grouped to at most one per 10 seconds during heavy
    activity.

## What happens on update

- **No re-pairing.** The existing pairing, device, settings and every consent
  decision carry over. This was checked with Dalamud's real serializer, upgrading
  the published 1.0.31.2 DLL to this exact 1.0.31.6 DLL: all serialized fields,
  OFF and ON preference sets, the pairing and a restart are preserved.
- **No permission prompt.** No setting or consent default changes, and no new
  data category is shared. Inventory uploads add container-coverage fields
  (observed, carried and unobserved locations, and which retainers' bags were
  read). An unopened saddlebag is uploaded from its last loaded contents instead
  of being dropped. Permissions stay as decided on Gillions.
- **New one-time chat notice:** if the character you are playing isn't linked, or
  isn't the one the device was paired with, Game Sync prints one chat line
  explaining what to do.
- **Status labels change** to the clearer wording above.

## What stays unavailable on gillions.app for now

- **FATEs:** show "Not available on Gillions" (or "Temporarily unavailable" if
  Gillions gives no FATE answer), no longer "Update required", until
  Gillions admits the 2026.10.16 FATE source. That needs Scribe's 2026.10.16
  FATE reference first.
- **Private Hunt bills and submarines, Hunt coverage, and travel:** unavailable
  until Gillions admits their 2026.10.16 reference data on Production.
- **Dashboard facts and Beastmaster:** are Testing-only diagnostics, not part of
  Stable.
