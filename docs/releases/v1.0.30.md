# Gillions Game Sync 1.0.30

This update keeps read-only character and Retainer synchronization, removes venture planning and AutoRetainer integration, and corrects pairing, record retention and redundant background work.

## What changes

- Pair once after updating. New credentials stay bound to their issuing HTTPS origin, and new records belong to that pairing and character. Older unverified local history stays preserved and inactive; website history is unchanged.
- Delayed acknowledgements preserve newer event evidence. Sale confirmation preserves other retainers' records and leaves equal-value ambiguity unresolved.
- Pending storage is shared across characters and pairing generations: 10,000 records or 8 MiB of serialized data. Admitted records are kept. At capacity, new event recording pauses with a visible gap warning until acknowledged drainage makes room. Missed events cannot be recovered, and re-pairing does not free old storage.
- The opening window focuses on connection, pairing, Automatic sync, manual sync and the separate website item-link control. Technical status, history and diagnostics are collapsed. Pairing destinations and material warnings stay visible.
- Retainer observation and upload scheduling no longer reset unrelated resource rotation. Freshness-only saves are coalesced; unchanged result views avoid repeated event construction. The two-second Gil fallback and 750 ms dirty delay are retained.
- Requests reject stale completions after character/pairing/lifecycle changes. System evidence is gated before extraction, replies are bounded and validated, and remote error text is not echoed. Pairing no longer submits machine identifiers.
- Removes AutoRetainer discovery/IPC, plan delivery and apply/restore controls. Opaque legacy backups and ownership records survive, including timestamp strings with their original offsets and precision. No automatic restore, cleanup or recovery/export feature is added.

## Updating

Keep the existing stable custom repository URL and update through Dalamud:

`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anndrox/GillionsGameSync/main/data/GillionsGameSync.json`

Open Gillions Game Sync, verify the displayed pairing destination and enter a fresh one-time code from the website. Do not delete the configuration. Valid pairings created by this update can resume after later reloads without another pairing.

Automatic sync is one global control for supported categories. Pairing and paired startup still perform one character sync and presence request even when recurring sync is off. Website item links have a separate control, on by default. Retainer uploads continue to require server product/contract acceptance.

This update does not cancel plans already installed in AutoRetainer or prove cancellation of callbacks queued by an older loaded plugin. Manage external automation through its own controls.

The candidate is verified with local stable/testing builds, synthetic policies and actual configuration serializer fixtures. Those checks do not establish live-game or rendered UI acceptance. Independent review, public availability and an individual user's installed version remain separate states. Preserved legacy history and nonpending snapshots are outside the pending-data budget; total configuration size and player coverage hours are not promised.
